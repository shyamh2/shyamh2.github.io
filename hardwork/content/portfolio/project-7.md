#### Project Description
This study focuses on the implementation of a solar microgrid to meet the load of charging electric vehicles in a residential community in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The system is proposed for a building of thirty six households, with an estimated load of 252 kWh/day. The goal is to provide uninterrupted electricity with zero carbon emissions for the specific and stated intention of charging EVs.
