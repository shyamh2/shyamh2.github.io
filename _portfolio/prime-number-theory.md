During my first Thanksgiving break, an interesting question came into my mind when playing video games with my cousin: how many controls are possible with a simple controller?

This short paper documents that effort and a result about the form of the prime numbers. I have come to realise that the answer to the above question presented is incomplete as it does not account for certain types of video game controls (long pressing a button, 360 degrees of freedom with the sticks).
